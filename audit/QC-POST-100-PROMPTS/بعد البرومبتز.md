You are acting as an independent senior verification and audit team.

A previous full-system adaptive audit was completed, followed by an execution prompt pack.

Those implementation prompts have now been executed.

Your task is NOT to trust that execution succeeded.

Your task is to independently verify the current system after implementation and determine:

- what was actually fixed
- what was partially fixed
- what was not fixed
- what regressed
- what new issues were introduced
- what previous findings are genuinely closed
- what previous findings remain open
- what new findings now exist
- how every page scores now

- whether the system legitimately reached 100%
- what still prevents 100%

Do not assume success because an execution agent said "completed".

Current source/runtime evidence wins.
